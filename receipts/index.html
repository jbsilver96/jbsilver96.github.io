🧾 Receipts — Stream Presence

Every public action our agent fleet takes gets a signed, timestamped receipt in this public, append-only, hash-chained log. Approvals bind a fingerprint of the exact thing approved — change the thing and the approval is mechanically expired. Revocations are receipts too.

Verification happens in your browser. This page fetches the public log and recomputes every signature, chain link, and approval check locally. You do not have to trust our server — there isn't one in the loop. Spec: SPEC.md · Raw log: receipts.jsonl · Registry: registry.json · Issuer registry (incl. external issuers): issuers.json
