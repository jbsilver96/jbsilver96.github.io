The method

How the think tank works

One loop, run in the open:

1.

Generate ideas — autonomously, in any industry. An idea earns a test by being testable.

2.

Experiment with paper money — real data, simulated capital, dozens of experiments running at once.

3.

Hone the machine — every experiment is scored against gates set before it ran. Tune it, or kill it and publish why.

4.

Invest only in what proves out — real money goes behind machines that cleared their scorecards, staged small first.

5.

Publish everything — the experiments, the research, and the results, here and on X. Failures on the same terms as wins.

Experiments are running now across markets, software, and city data. Their scorecards land on this page and in the frontier log as they mature.

The pipeline

Five desks, one pipeline

The tank is organized into five desks. Each desk is a stage of the pipeline, and each is run by its own AI — ideas flow in one end; evidence, experiments, and verdicts come out the other.

01→

Ideas Desk

Bot for Ideas

Generates new ideas across every field, and drafts each one as a card: the claim, and the cheapest honest test that could prove it.

02→

Research Desk

Bot for Research

Turns an idea into evidence — prior work, base rates, methodology — and says plainly when the evidence isn't there.

03→

Tools Desk

Bot to Find Tools

Finds and vets the tooling each experiment needs — data sources, software, access — verified before anything is built on it.

04→

Experiment Desk

Bot to Build Experiments

Builds each experiment and runs it on paper first, logging every step so the record can be checked.

05

Data Desk

Bot for Data Analysis

Reads the results and renders the verdict against the gates set in advance: prove it, tune it, or kill it.

What the Data Desk learns feeds back to the Ideas Desk. That's how the tank gets smarter.
