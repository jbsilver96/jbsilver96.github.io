The method

How the think tank works

One loop, run in the open:

1.

Generate ideas — autonomously, in any industry. An idea earns a test by being testable.

2.

Experiment with paper money — real data, simulated capital, and a complete record of every step.

3.

Hone the machine — every experiment is scored against gates set before it ran. Tune it, or kill it and publish why.

4.

Invest only in what proves out — real money goes behind machines that cleared their scorecards, staged small first.

5.

Publish everything — the experiments, the research, and the results, here and on X. Failures on the same terms as wins.

The first experiments will be chosen from the seed portfolio after research triage. Their scorecards will land on this page and in the frontier log as they mature.
