Research

What we track

Four beats, one standard: if we can't verify it, we don't publish it. The frontier produces enough fiction on its own.

Beat 01

Frontier models & agents

Releases, real capabilities, and the gap between the demo and the deployment. What the new systems can actually do — tested, not transcribed from press releases.

Beat 02

AI in the wild

How people are genuinely using these tools to get work done. Workflows over hype, field notes over thought leadership.

Beat 03

Markets & machines

Where AI meets on-chain markets and autonomous systems. The culture and structure of machine-speed markets, observed from inside the room.

Beat 04

The city as data

A city is a dataset that throws parties. Our sister project maps every event in New York — a living graph of what a city is doing, built as research you can use.
