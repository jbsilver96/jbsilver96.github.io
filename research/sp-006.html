Section 1

Decision and exact target

2026-10-22. Expands section 5 of the earlier triage. Research and bounded access precheck only. No replication experiment was approved, launched, or completed. No paid compute was provisioned.

WORTH AN EXPERIMENT. Specifically, a capped raw-reconstruction gate followed only on success by a preregistered replication. The reason is now stronger than “public data exist”: two official calibrated L2 CDFs were actually retrieved and parsed, and the archived implementation exposes concrete, testable reproducibility questions. This is not a finding that the physical claim is false.

Version-pinned target: Iizawa et al., arXiv 2507.13213v2, dated 2026-10-13, arxiv.org/html/… . It reports opposite-signed spectral peaks in single-spacecraft reduced radial magnetic polarization, with lower-frequency migration across distance. It is not a universal solar-wind handedness rule. Its data window is 2021-09-29 00:00 to 2021-11-22 00:00 UTC. At 0.1 AU it describes negative and positive peaks near 10 and 20 Hz. Its exponential curve is explicitly a rough guide, not a fitted parameter estimate.

The measured quantity is sigma(f) = 2 Im[C_TN(f)] / (P_TT(f) + P_NN(f)), with C_TN = mean(conj(FFT(B_T)) × FFT(B_N)), and exp(-2 pi i f t) Fourier convention. Retain the transverse denominator, not P_RR + P_TT + P_NN. It describes rotation around the radial axis; it is not full 3D helicity or a directly measured helicity flux.

Two separate questions:

  • Primary: do opposite-signed lobes occur within the same qualified held-out hour more often than under the registered noise/sampling null?
  • Secondary: do their frequencies migrate relative to local scales after correcting time joins, sampling and plasma-condition confounds?

Finding a pair answers only the first. Neither outcome establishes a causal inverse cascade, a helicity barrier, a unique wave mode, or evolution of the same plasma parcel. A second-order single-spacecraft statistic cannot by itself establish those mechanisms.

Material reproducibility findings

These are static code findings plus direct checks of archived arrays, not a raw-data test of the physical conclusions. The source claims to generate these figures, but its exact execution lineage into the deposited arrays is not verified. The array dimensions and CI pattern corroborate specific observations, not every code path.

1. Welch length mismatch. The paper's Fig3 caption says 8192 points; archived calc_mag_helicity_normalized_welch uses 2048 with 50% overlap. HDF5 frequency grids contain 1025 one-sided bins, consistent with 2048, not 8192. Magnitude PSD uses 8192. Calculation lines 412–425; reconcile rather than choose the favorable variant. 2. Significance problem. The archived signed-sigma confidence bounds multiply sigma by positive chi-square ratios. Therefore a nonzero estimate cannot obtain an interval crossing zero under that construction. Direct array check: all 8,184 finite nonzero Fig3 entries have bounds on the same side of zero. This is a mathematical defect for using “CI excludes zero” as a helicity sign/null test. It does not establish whether the visible peaks survive a valid test. Chi-square power intervals can be appropriate for nonnegative autospectral power under stated assumptions; that rationale does not justify applying positive scale factors to a signed cross-spectral ratio. Use independently validated uncertainty. Calculation lines 427–441. 3. Cadence-path mismatch. Fig3's 0.2 AU array stops at 4.577622749 Hz, while the Fig4 0.2 AU array reaches about 73.24 Hz. Fig3 and Fig4 use separate IPC loaders. Also the 0.1 AU Fig3 grid corresponds to 0.003415 s, versus 0.003414 s in the main grid. Calculation loader lines 152–180. The paper also prints 3441 microseconds near 0.1 AU, versus 3414/3415 microseconds in source paths. Do not silently correct or equate these. Unsupported frequencies are not physical nondetections. 4. Plasma-row indexing risk. In calc_figure4 the density and speed arrays span the full concatenated distance grid, but the index i restarts within each cadence group. Later groups use mean_densities_interp[i] and mean_flow_speeds_interp[i] without the group offset. That code path pairs some spectra with earlier plasma rows. Register raw cyclotron and propagation-model normalizations separately. Correcting a mislabeled axis is not automatically a physical refutation. 6. Quality/selection risks. The supplied MAG converter retains magnetic values and timestamps, not the quality bit field. The spline step has no explicit maximum-gap guard. Section 7

Frozen estimator and primary peak statistic

Everything below is a proposed preregistration, not a completed analysis. Numeric tolerances are study design choices, not established instrument standards. Freeze software, configuration and cohort hashes before examining held-out helicity.

Use independent Hann Welch PSD/CSD, 64-second segments, 50% overlap, constant detrending, one-sided density scaling, and C_TN=conj(T)×N. Explicitly construct the periodic Hann array instead of inheriting a version-sensitive default. Retain native cadence and round segment length to the nearest integer sample, recording the actual duration. Use sigma's transverse denominator. The reference reproduction branch separately retains the authors' 2048/8192 alternatives; do not mix that branch with held-out inference.

Primary scope: 0.100≤r<0.120 AU, fixed spacecraft-frequency bands L=[6,14) Hz and H=[14,30] Hz. These bands deliberately test a narrow near-Sun recurrence claim. A feature shifted outside them is a failure of this bounded criterion, not proof that no bihelical feature exists anywhere.

Exact peak statistic:

  • Freeze candidate log-window centers c_j=6×2^(j/6) Hz for integer j≥0. Each window spans [c_j/2^(1/6), c_j×2^(1/6)]. Retain only windows wholly inside L or H. No data-dependent smoothing or band adjustment.
  • For each candidate window w calculate a_w=2×sum(Im C_TN×df)/sum((P_TT+P_NN)×df). This is a ratio of integrated spectra, not an unweighted average of noisy normalized bins.
  • For every low/high window pair compute q=max(min(-a_L,a_H), min(a_L,-a_H)); Q_i is the maximum q for hour i. The peak locations are the winning fixed-window centers; ties go to the lower frequency, then earlier center. Q_i>0 requires opposite signs in the SAME hour, with size equal to the weaker lobe. Record the raw signed amplitudes and all candidate scores.
  • For encounter e, T_e=max_i Q_i. This is an occurrence endpoint, not an estimate that all hours or all solar wind are bihelical.

Proposed primary success: in EACH of E11 and E12, at least one eligible hour has Q_i≥0.20 and simultaneous confidence intervals for the two winning lobe amplitudes lie on opposite sides of zero. Use the simultaneous all-window intervals and Bonferroni allocation over all selected hours in BOTH encounters specified below, targeting nominal familywise error 5%. Their coverage protects both orientations and any selected pair without independence assumptions between hours. Repeat the complete maximization in every synthetic calibration realization. No uncorrected peak picking.

A broad band-average version is one declared sensitivity, not a replacement endpoint if this fails. Frequency bins are not independent replicates. Report failures and unsupported windows explicitly. Inward/outward sector signs remain visible; no post hoc sign flip to improve agreement.

Cross-spectrum convention source: docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/…/scipy.signal.csd.html .

Section 9

Frozen uncertainty and calibration

Proposed, NOT validated. Freeze this algorithm, code, seeds and block length at the access gate using original-period pilots; then validate under the follow-on cap before held-out unblinding. Failure stops inference or requires an amended, refrozen plan, not silent method switching.

For every accepted 64 s Welch segment, retain jointly all candidate-window integrated imaginary cross-power numerators and transverse-power denominators. Resample these complete vectors together, preserving component/window relationships and overlap dependence. Use a moving-block bootstrap within each hour: uniformly draw all eligible overlapping blocks of L consecutive segment vectors, concatenate draws and truncate to the original number of segments. Blocks cannot cross a rejected segment, gap or regime boundary. Recompute each ratio a_w and the selected pair for every resample; never bootstrap already selected peaks alone.

Pilot rule: from original-period segment numerator/denominator series, find the first lag after which absolute autocorrelation is below 0.1 for three successive lags, taking the largest across nonconstant windows/series. Choose the smallest L in {4,8} at least twice that lag and freeze it globally. No such L, fewer than eight block-equivalents in an hour, or too few eligible contiguous blocks means inadequate uncertainty support, not a smaller convenient block. This finite-sample recipe must pass calibration; it is not an established instrument standard.

Use B=19,999 bootstrap draws per hour, seed 6006. For draw b calculate D_b=max_w |a*_w−a_w| across ALL candidate windows. Allocate alpha_h=0.05/48 to each hour, using the hard 48-hour maximum across BOTH encounters even if fewer survive. Sort D; take the order statistic at rank ceil((B+1)(1−alpha_h)), capped at B. Every window gets [a_w−c_h,a_w+c_h], clipped to [−1,1]. This nonstudentized simultaneous-band construction plus Bonferroni over hours is the one primary method. No

assumption of independent hours is needed for the union bound, but bootstrap coverage remains an assumption to validate. Cross-hour descriptive intervals use fixed six-hour clusters, with 12/24-hour sensitivity where supported; two encounters do not establish population universality.

Calibration uses synthetic multicomponent signals and real allowed timestamps/masks. Include colored zero polarization, one polarized band, two same-sign bands, opposite-sign lobes, instrumental lines, clipping, current-sheet steps, low SNR, jitter/interpolation and edge drift. Maximum four frozen representative cadence/mask classes. For each, run 2,000 composite-null cases and 1,000 injected alternatives: 500 at true weaker-lobe amplitude 0.30 and 125 each at 0, 0.10, 0.20 and 0.40. Each realization is a full mock cohort of 48 hours: alternatives place one pair in a fixed hour per encounter, all others null. Inject at c_4 and c_10 with the registered window width, stronger-lobe amplitude 0.50, and balance both sign orientations. Freeze noise levels/masks on original-period pilots. Truth means integrated window amplitude, not raw sinusoid amplitude.

Repeat the full endpoint, including Q≥0.20 and simultaneous sign intervals. Require empirical false positives ≤0.05 and their one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound ≤0.065 under every null class. Require the one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson lower bound on power ≥0.80 at the 0.30 alternative and median amplitude bias ≤0.03. Report the complete power curve by noise/coverage class. There is NO 80% power requirement at true amplitude 0.20, which is the observed-effect decision boundary. Monte Carlo tolerances are proposed checks, not proof of exact finite-sample error control. Stop if calibration or compute caps fail.

Controls: preserve raw polarity, leg, sampling angle and Vrel; audit noise/lines and sensor dependence (merged MAG/SCM is not independent MAG validation); show gaps, jitter, stationarity and switchback cuts; join quality-screened SWEAP by UTC, not row index.

Spacecraft-frequency inference needs no Taylor conversion. For spatial claims use Vrel=Vplasma−Vspacecraft in verified RTN, VA and epsilon=delta-u/(sqrt(2) Vperp). Proposed strict subset: epsilon≤0.3 and VA/Vperp≤0.3 with measured nonzero Vperp. These screens do not prove validity for dispersive modes. Report phase-speed/angle sensitivity or stop at frequency-only conclusions. arxiv.org/abs/1406.5470 .

Section 12

Results, publication bar and final call

Keep these outcomes separate:

ACCESS/RECONSTRUCTION FAILURE: original window identity, raw/calibration versions, exact convention, or reference spectrum cannot be reconciled within the gate. Produce the manifest and discrepancy log. No physical refutation claim.

INADEQUATE/INCONCLUSIVE: access succeeds, but qualified coverage, noise documentation, synthetic false-positive control or power does not pass. Report the missing sensitivity and do not search extra encounters until something turns positive.

ATTENUATION: valid reprocessing/control comparisons reduce the

same measured lobe amplitudes or detection evidence. Quantify paired changes and uncertainty. A point estimate dropping is not enough to claim statistically established attenuation, and nuisance removal can remove real shared signal.

PRIMARY NONREPLICATION: access and sensitivity pass, but the preregistered paired-peak criterion fails in one or both held-out encounters. State the exact radius/frequency/amplitude scope and uncertainty. This does not refute the existential observation that a pair occurred in the original data.

PRIMARY REPLICATION: the frozen occurrence criterion passes independently in both held-out encounters with simultaneous error control and documented controls. This supports recurring reduced radial polarization pairs, not ubiquity.

EXTENDED REPLICATION: primary occurrence plus separately supported local-scale-normalized migration under valid mapping and geometry controls. If primary passes but migration does not, report that split explicitly.

NARROW REFUTATION: reserve this stronger word for a specific registered quantitative assertion that is incompatible with a valid reconstruction or a powered/equivalence-bounded result. For example, if a new preregistration asserts an original-period lobe ≥0.20, a valid upper bound below 0.20 could contradict that quantitative assertion. This 0.20 threshold is ours, not a reported paper threshold. Do not attach “refuted” to the whole paper from code inspection, one null encounter, a failed download, or a p-value above 0.05. The currently inspected CI construction is invalid for the proposed zero-exclusion inference; the underlying physical claim has not been tested here.

Publishable unit: a versioned raw-file manifest, minimal independently implemented reader/estimator, immutable cohort/configuration, raw-to-spectrum lineage, synthetic validation and power surfaces, selected and failed spectra, valid uncertainty, control/exclusion tables, corrected-versus-reference discrepancies, and an encounter-specific report. Release code and compact derived results with source/archive attribution, not an undocumented cache of figures. Retain raw provider links and hashes so others can retrieve exact inputs.

Publication bar: a technically reviewed result that changes confidence in the physical inference, demonstrates a reproducibility defect with its actual effect, or gives a sufficiently powered independent replication. Merely rerendering the authors' HDF5 or listing suspected bugs is not automatically a journal paper. A negative result is informative only with access, sensitivity, scope and exclusions documented. Specialist interpretation is especially important for noise and Taylor/geometry issues. Publication acceptance and novelty are not guaranteed.
