What Frugl does

Three things, all automatic. You don't rebuild anything, and your users never see a difference.

1

Plug in — change one line

Point your app at Frugl instead of your AI provider. That's the whole setup. Frugl works with the major model APIs, and your provider account stays yours.

2

We shrink every request

Bulky data is re-written in a compact form and compressed automatically before it's sent. Nothing you send is ever lost — the originals are kept and can be pulled back mid-conversation, automatically.

3

Same answers

Quality is checked, not hoped for. If shrinking a request could risk the answer, Frugl passes it through untouched instead. Savings never eat the evidence.

How it works

Your app talks to Frugl. Frugl talks to your AI model. The answers come back exactly as before.

Your app→ Frugl→ Your AI model
# Before — you call the model directly base_url = "https://api.your-provider.com/v1" # After — one line changes, nothing else does base_url = "https://your-frugl-endpoint/v1" # your Frugl endpoint

Same code, same keys, same responses. Just smaller requests.
